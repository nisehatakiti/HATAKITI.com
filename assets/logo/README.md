# HATAKITI.com Logo

## Main Logo

`hatakiti-main.png` is the official main logo of HATAKITI.com.

The logo represents HATAKITI through stage equipment and theatrical lighting.

### Design concept

The word `HATAKITI` is constructed using equipment and materials commonly found in theatre production, including:

* Stage pipes and fittings
* Ladder
* Stage curtains
* Apple box / 箱馬
* Stands and poles
* Gaffer tape / 養生テープ
* Other stage production equipment

The logo is illuminated from both sides with contrasting stage lights, representing the theatrical background of HATAKITI.

### Usage

* Use this logo as the primary visual identity of HATAKITI.com.
* The logo should not include a subtitle or tagline.
* Use the main logo prominently in the website header or other primary branding locations.
* Do not replace the logo with a generic corporate-style logo.
* StageArt has its own identity and should not be incorporated into this logo.
