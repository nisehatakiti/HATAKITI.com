## Navigation principles

### HATAKITIとは

A human-facing introduction to the author, background, interests, and ongoing activities. StageArt may be introduced here, but remains a separate system.

### 日々の所感

A free-form blog area. It should support ordinary posts without forcing the author into a fixed record schema.

### 演劇

A structured knowledge area intended to grow into a practical theatre textbook / knowledge base. Topics should be navigable independently from the chronological blog.

### 観劇記録

A structured archive of theatre and performance viewing records.

### 映画記録

A structured archive of film viewing records.

### 検索・アーカイブ

Search and archive functions should allow retrieval by date, category, people, groups, works, and tags where data exists.

The archive should support discovering older records rather than only showing the newest posts.
