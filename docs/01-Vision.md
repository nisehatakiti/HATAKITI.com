# HATAKITI.com Blueprint

# 01 - Vision

Version : 1.2

---

## 1. Purpose

HATAKITI.com is a personal record, knowledge, and archive site centered on the author's own thoughts, theatre activity, viewing experiences, and accumulated knowledge.

It is a personal website and archive, not a product portal or corporate-style media site.

StageArt is a separate product and system. HATAKITI.com may introduce and link to StageArt as one of the author's activities, but StageArt data, authentication, domain model, and application infrastructure are not part of HATAKITI.com.

---

## 2. Brand / Logo

The HATAKITI.com main logo is confirmed as the theatrical equipment wordmark concept developed during the project.

The logo expresses the name HATAKITI using familiar stage / theatre equipment and backstage materials, including items such as ladders, stage pipes, curtains, stands, apple boxes, and gaffer tape.

The confirmed main visual treatment uses opposing stage lighting from the left and right sides, creating a theatrical atmosphere and emphasizing the backstage / stagecraft character of HATAKITI.

The main logo does not use a subtitle or tagline.

The logo should be treated as the primary HATAKITI.com visual identity unless a future explicit design decision replaces it.

Recommended repository asset path:

```text
assets/logo/hatakiti-main.png
```

## 3. What the site records

HATAKITI.com combines four kinds of content.

### 3.1 Free-form thought

Daily observations, ideas, essays, opinions, notes, and other writing are recorded in a conventional blog format.

This area should remain flexible. The author should be able to write without being forced into a structured data-entry form.

### 3.2 Theatre viewing records

Viewing experiences are recorded in a simple, consistent format so that the author can look back on what was watched.

The purpose is primarily personal record and readability, not building a comprehensive theatre database.

### 3.3 Film viewing records

Film viewing experiences are recorded in a simple, consistent format for later reference.

Again, the purpose is to preserve the author's own viewing history and thoughts rather than to construct a large external film database.

### 3.4 Theatre notes / acting thoughts

The theatre section records the author's own practical observations and ideas about theatre and acting.

This is not intended to become a formal academic textbook or comprehensive acting school curriculum. It should contain relatively basic, useful ideas that come from the author's own experience and way of thinking.

Examples include:

- dialogue comes from emotion
- stage position and body direction can be understood as vectors of emotion
- physical movement can become clearer when the actor consciously considers when to stop
- relationships between actors can be expressed through distance and orientation
- volume of voice and intensity of emotion are not necessarily the same thing

The section should remain concise, practical, personal, and easy to read.

---

## 4. Core principle

HATAKITI.com should feel like a record of a person rather than a database built for its own sake.

The site should make it easy to understand:

- what HATAKITI is interested in
- what HATAKITI has watched
- what HATAKITI has thought about
- what HATAKITI has learned through theatre and other creative activity
- what HATAKITI is currently working on

Search, categories, and archives are useful supporting functions, but the site should not become overly complicated simply to create relationships between every piece of content.

Content does not need to be heavily cross-linked. A viewing record can stand on its own, a personal essay can stand on its own, and a theatre note can stand on its own.

---

## 5. StageArt positioning

StageArt should appear on HATAKITI.com as one of the author's activities and creations.

The relationship should be subtle rather than advertising-heavy.

Possible placements include:

- a small introduction in HATAKITIとは / 活動・制作
- occasional references in relevant personal posts
- a modest StageArt introduction or banner/link
- a direct link to the independent StageArt site

HATAKITI.com should never become a StageArt portal.

The intended relationship is:

```text
HATAKITI.com
  ├─ HATAKITI自身について
  ├─ 日々の所感
  ├─ 観劇記録
  ├─ 映画記録
  ├─ 演劇・演技についての個人的な考え
  └─ StageArt（活動・制作の一つとして紹介）
                         │
                         └─→ StageArt（独立したプロダクト）
```

StageArt should have a clear link from HATAKITI.com, while remaining technically and conceptually independent.

---

## 6. Long-term character

The site should feel personal rather than corporate.

The purpose is not to present an abstract media brand, but to leave a searchable record of what the author watched, thought about, learned, and created over time.

The archive should remain useful years after an individual article or viewing record was created.

At the same time, simplicity is more important than building a sophisticated knowledge graph. HATAKITI.com should grow naturally through accumulated writing and records rather than through a requirement to maintain complex relationships between entities.
