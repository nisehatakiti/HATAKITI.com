# HATAKITI.com Blueprint

# 01 - Vision

Version : 1.0

---

## 1. Purpose

HATAKITI.com is a personal record, knowledge, and archive site centered on the author's own thoughts, theatre activity, viewing experiences, and accumulated knowledge.

It is intentionally independent from StageArt.

StageArt is a separate product and system. HATAKITI.com may introduce or link to StageArt as one of the author's activities, but StageArt data, authentication, domain model, and application infrastructure are not part of HATAKITI.com.

---

## 2. What the site records

HATAKITI.com combines three kinds of content.

### 2.1 Free-form thought

Daily observations, ideas, essays, opinions, notes, and other writing are recorded in a conventional blog format.

### 2.2 Structured records

Viewing experiences are recorded through fixed forms so that they can later be searched, filtered, and connected.

The initial structured records are:

- Theatre / performance viewing records
- Film viewing records

### 2.3 Knowledge / textbook content

The theatre section is intended to grow into a practical, structured introduction to theatre and acting rather than being only a collection of essays.

The knowledge section may eventually cover acting, voice, body, script, direction, rehearsal, stagecraft, and other theatre-related subjects.

---

## 3. Core principle

HATAKITI.com should be designed as an archive that becomes more useful as more records accumulate.

A visitor should be able to move between:

- a viewing record
- a work / production
- a theatre group
- a performer
- a theatre topic
- a free-form essay

when those relationships are available.

The site therefore should not be designed as a simple chronological blog only.

---

## 4. Long-term character

The site should feel personal rather than corporate.

The purpose is not to present an abstract media brand, but to leave a searchable record of what the author watched, thought about, learned, and created over time.

The archive should remain useful years after an individual article or viewing record was created.
